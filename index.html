SmartBuild AI — Construction Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered architectural CAD and cost-estimation platform for Sri Lanka's residential construction market — bridging design, CIDA-indexed quantity surveying, and bank loan feasibility in one workflow.

  • 2D/3D architectural studio (Three.js) generated from land size and room counts, plus a computer-vision floor-plan analyzer
  • CIDA-indexed Bill of Quantities with XGBoost price forecasting across all 25 districts
  • 45% DSR bank-loan feasibility matrix comparing BOC, Commercial Bank, HNB, Sampath & NSB
  • Bilingual (Sinhala/English) Gemini-powered civil engineering consultant
View on GitHub

Why XGBoost over a simpler model: district-level material costs move non-linearly with distance from Colombo ports and local supply constraints — gradient boosting handled that better than linear regression in testing.

Why a rules-based DSR matrix instead of one blended rate: each bank prices risk differently, so a single "average" feasibility number would hide the deals that are actually financeable.

Open question: keeping the floor-plan computer-vision analyzer accurate across hand-drawn vs. CAD-exported plans is still the hardest part of this build.
